How to use it

Dump Fun is a web page. Open it, pick a coin, sign the trade in your own wallet.

  1. Open Dump FunOn your phone, your PC or any browser next to the TV. Add it to your home screen if you want an app icon.
  2. Plug in a controllerOn a PC or Steam Deck, any pad works: D-pad to move, ✕ to trade, ○ to go back. On a phone, just tap.
  3. Connect PhantomHit Trade, then connect your wallet in the swap box. Dump Fun never sees your keys.
  4. Buy or sellThe swap is routed by Jupiter. You review it and sign it in Phantom. That is the whole flow.

Straight answers

FAQ

Does it work on the PS5?

The PS5 has no official web browser, so the console itself is not a supported screen. Use your phone, a PC or a smart TV browser beside it.

Is it custodial? Do I need an account?

No account and no custody. Trades go through Jupiter's swap widget and you sign every one in your own wallet. Dump Fun has no backend and stores nothing.

Where does the data come from?

Dexscreener's public API. Dump Fun shows Solana tokens whose address ends in "pump", the pump.fun mint suffix. Trending lists include promoted tokens, so do your own research.

Is this affiliated with pump.fun, Rockstar or Sony?

No. Dump Fun is an independent open-source project and a parody of the pump.fun name. GTA VI is a trademark of Take-Two Interactive.
